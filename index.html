01 · flagship · KKBox WSDM Cup

Churn, predictedbefore it happens.

970,960 subscribers, ~50 engineered features, and one uncomfortable question: does the model secretly know the future?

Three tables — labels, demographics, two years of transactions — joined into 970,960 subscribers at a 9% churn rate, described by ~50 engineered behavioral and tenure features.

Then the audit: every time-based feature checked against a strict 2017-03-31 cutoff — catching a date-parsing flaw that leaked future tenure as “active status”. Cleaned, LightGBM reaches 0.956 PR AUC, and the top 10% risk slice holds 94.8% of churners.

02 · NLP + risk · CFPB corpus

Complaints that warnbefore they explode.

The CFPB consumer-complaint database, turned into a live risk-intelligence system.

Every complaint is a signal; the problem is volume. TF-IDF compressed into a TruncatedSVD semantic space, a classifier with calibrated probabilities, and cosine-similarity retrieval matching historical cases in ~0.18ms — no GPU required.

Serving is engineered, not implied: a FastAPI REST server, a Streamlit interface, and SQL analysis of complaint patterns and dispute rates — plus an anomaly radar that notices when the shape of risk changes.

03 · systems · NSE tickers

Two engines,one honest forecast.

Two models disagree about the market. The engine listens to both — and to the macro news everyone else is reading.

XGBoost and an Attention LSTM, each trained on 15-day sequential price action for NSE tickers and cached for high-throughput inference.

Gemini 2.5 Flash synthesizes macro drivers — RBI repo rates, Union Budget, tariffs — blended at a deliberate 60/40. Every inference and macro summary is persisted for historical lookup.
